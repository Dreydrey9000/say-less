### Studio hotkeys that open the right page

Say Less can now be told which Studio page to open from outside the app. Run
`/Applications/Say Less.app/Contents/MacOS/handy --open-studio titles` (or `image`, `recordings`,
`screens`, `library`) from a hotkey tool such as Hammerspoon, Raycast or
Shortcuts, and the Studio opens on that page, whether Say Less is already
running or closed.
