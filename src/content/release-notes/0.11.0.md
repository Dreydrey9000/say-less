### A little more personality. A lot less cleanup.

Your new **Home** screen puts shortcuts, recent dictation, and your floating companion together.

- In **Appearance**, choose Orbit, Helix, Wave, or Chrome S. Cycle formations every 20 seconds, pause motion, and place the dock on either screen edge. Drag its grip to position it freely. Reduced Motion keeps it still.
- In **Writing**, choose **Sentence formatting**, add your own spelling corrections, and enable **AI cleanup for normal dictation** to use your configured Post Process provider on the regular shortcut. If that provider is unavailable, local formatting still works and the app tells you.
- In **General**, choose **Auto**: hold to talk, tap to record hands-free, or double-tap to lock recording. Press again to finish.
