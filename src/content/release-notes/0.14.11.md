### Pick your dock from a gallery

Open **Appearance → Dock look** to see the five looks side by side. Scroll sideways and choose a card. Your choice is remembered. You can also focus the selected card and use the arrow keys.

![The horizontal gallery with previews of all five dock looks](/release-notes/0.14.11/dock-gallery.png)

The floating dock now opens without changing the app's macOS window policy. Use **Show floating dock** on Home or in Appearance.
