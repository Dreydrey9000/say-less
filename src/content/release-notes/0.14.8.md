### The small dock shows what Say Less is doing

The small floating dock grows into a short status island while it listens, turns speech into text, or records your screen. When a screen recording finishes saving, it shows **Saved** and a folder button to find the video. It becomes small again when idle.

![The small Say Less dock expanded into a Listening status island, with hide, screen, and stop controls visible](/release-notes/0.14.8/small-dock-listening.png "The dock expands while listening and returns to the small companion when idle.")

Click the emblem to open the full dock, or drag it to move the small dock anywhere on screen. The X hides it when recording is stopped. Turn off animations in Appearance or use your system's Reduce Motion setting for a still version.
