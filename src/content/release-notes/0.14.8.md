### The small dock shows what Say Less is doing

The small floating dock grows into a short status island while it listens, turns speech into text, or records your screen. When a screen recording finishes saving, it shows **Saved** and a folder button to find the video. It becomes small again when idle.

![The small Say Less dock showing Listening with an attached Stop segment](/release-notes/0.14.8/small-dock-listening.png "The dock expands while listening and returns to the small companion when idle.")

Click the emblem to open the full dock, or drag it anywhere on screen. On Mac, drop near a side or corner to snap it there. You can also choose a position in **Appearance → Dock placement**.

Hover over the emblem or reach it with Tab to reveal the attached screen and hide controls. While recording, Stop stays visible; after a video saves, the folder button opens its location. Turn off animations in Appearance or use your system's Reduce Motion setting for a still version.
