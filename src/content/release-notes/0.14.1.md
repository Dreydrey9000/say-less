### Record your screen

Needs macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer. Windows is coming soon.

Say Less records your screen, your voice and your computer's sound into one video. It can also put your face in a small circle in the corner. Say Less itself stays out of the shot.

**To start:** click **Record your screen** at the top of Home, then **Record screen**. You can also click **Screen** on the floating dock, pick **Record Screen** in the menu bar, or press your dictation shortcut and say **"Say less, start recording."**

**To add your face:** click **Setup** next to **Record screen** and turn on **Show your face**. Pick the corner and the size. The same panel picks your mic, turns your computer's sound on or off, and can record one window instead of the whole screen.

**To stop:** click **Stop**. Your video is saved in the **Say Less** folder in **Movies**. Click **Open recordings folder** on Home any time to find it.

The first time, macOS asks for Screen Recording permission. After you turn it on in System Settings, click **Reopen Say Less** so it takes effect.

### What's better in this update

- The dock's dictation button now says **Talk** and the recording button says **Screen**, so you can tell them apart. While you record, the dock shows **Stop**.
- Home has a **Record your screen** link at the top, so you don't have to scroll to find it.
- If you said no to a permission, the button now opens the right page in System Settings, and **Reopen Say Less** restarts the app for you.
- If we can't find a camera, we say so and tell you how to fix it or record without your face.
- Switches are easier to read. On is filled with your color.
- Say Less now checks for updates while it stays open and shows an **Update now** button. If an update fails, we tell you and link to the download.
- On a Mac older than macOS 15, we show how to check your version and update it.

### Avatars

Pick one of 16 painted characters for your recording pill and dock. Open **Appearance**, choose **Avatar** under the recording indicator, then pick one under **Pick a character**.
