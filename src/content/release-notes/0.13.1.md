### Voice visuals, polished

We rebuilt the moving parts so they feel smoother and look more like Say Less.

- **Squiggle** is now three soft strands in your color with a bright silver center. It swells as you talk, settles gently when you pause, and rests on a faint line so the pill never looks empty.
- **Your avatar** blinks on its own, looks around a little, breathes while it listens, and bounces when you start and stop. Its mouth now opens smoothly instead of jumping between shapes.
- **Pick your companion** in **Appearance** shows four different looks built from our silver S: Orbit, Helix, Wave, and the real **Chrome S**. The one you pick gets a check mark, and only it moves.
- **The small dock** has more depth, and its dot now tells you what is happening: gray when ready, bright while it works, amber if something went wrong.
- **The recording pill** is a dark, recessed silver pill in both light and dark mode.

Turn on **Pause animations** or your system's Reduce Motion and everything holds a still pose.
