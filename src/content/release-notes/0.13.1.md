### Clearer settings

Every setting now has one home, with plain names.

- **Home** tells you what to press: click where you want to type, then use your shortcut. It shows only the instruction for the way your shortcut works, and **Share Say Less** is right there.
- **New names:** General is now **Shortcuts & mic**, Models is **Speech engine**, Post Process is **AI cleanup**, and the pop-up while you talk is the **Recording indicator**.
- **Appearance** is laid out in clear sections: Color and theme, Floating dock, Recording indicator, and Motion. Settings that used to show up in two places now live in one, and the old spot links to it.
- **Dock look** is one choice. The particle patterns only show for looks that have particles.
- The bottom bar says which speech engine you use and whether it is ready, like "Nemotron · Ready".

### Easier setup

- First-time setup recommends **one** speech engine. The others are one click away.
- A new **Try it once** step lets you dictate into a practice box before you start, and shows how to stop the emoji picker from opening when you hold Fn.

### Voice visuals, polished

We rebuilt the moving parts so they feel smoother and look more like Say Less.

- **Voice line** (formerly Squiggle) is now three soft strands in your color with a bright silver center. It swells as you talk, settles gently when you pause, and rests on a faint line so the pill never looks empty.
- **Your avatar** blinks on its own, looks around a little, breathes while it listens, and bounces when you start and stop. Its mouth now opens smoothly instead of jumping between shapes.
- **Particle patterns** in **Appearance** show four different looks built from our silver S: Orbit, Helix, Wave, and the real **Chrome S**. The one you pick gets a check mark, and only it moves.
- **The small dock** has more depth, and its dot now tells you what is happening: gray when ready, bright while it works, amber if something went wrong.
- **The recording pill** is a dark, recessed silver pill in both light and dark mode.

Turn on **Pause animations** in Appearance, or your system's Reduce Motion, and everything holds a still pose. Recording keeps working either way.

### Easier to use, with or without a mouse

- **Change your shortcut from the keyboard.** In **Shortcuts & mic**, Tab to the shortcut and press Enter, then press the new keys. Press Escape to keep the old one.
- **The recording pill tells you what it is doing.** It says "Listening" while it hears you, and the avatar there is bigger, so you can see it talk. Its cancel button is bigger too.
- **Pausing animations freezes them in place** instead of jumping back to the start.
- **Buttons you click twice only act once,** so imports and saves never happen twice.
- **Clearer messages.** If your history can't load, we say so and let you try again. "Copy link" in **About** copies on the first click and says "Copied!". Removing a voice action can be undone.
- **Hover or Tab to any icon button** to see what it does.

### Final touches

- **The recording pill** is a little taller, with room around your avatar. The voice line, bars, or avatar now sit in their own space between "Listening" and the close button, so they never cover the words. The dot turns red while we listen.
- **The small dock** has a tiny status light inside its edge: silver when ready, red while recording, amber if something went wrong.
- **Setup** shows one clear button. If a speech engine is already on your computer, we suggest that one, so there is nothing to download. Each screen also says which step you are on.
- **Every dropdown** now looks the same and lines up with the text boxes next to it.
- **Insights** puts the number first, so you can see at a glance how often a topic comes up.
- **Shortcuts & mic** shows each setting's explanation right under its name, and reset buttons say **Reset**.
- **Light mode** uses a deeper shade of your color for thin lines like the menu highlight, so it stays easy to see.
