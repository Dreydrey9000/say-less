### Your creative tools, in one app

Open **Studio** in the Say Less sidebar for **Create**, **Titles**, **Videos**,
**Screens**, and **Library**. You can switch views while a job runs. Studio now
opens inside Say Less and its runtime receives the same desktop updates.

![Studio inside Say Less, with the five creative tools in one window](/release-notes/0.14.12/studio.png)

Open **Studio connections** to review your image and cloud tools. Image
generation and cloud upload still need connected tools and accounts. Local
recordings and saved images stay available without cloud storage. Automatic
uploads start off; turn them on only after setting up your B2 connection.

Existing libraries are imported without removing the originals. Restarting for
an update waits for your Studio jobs and uploads to finish or be cancelled.
