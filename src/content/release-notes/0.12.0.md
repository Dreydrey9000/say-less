### Your dock, pocket-sized

Use **Collapse to companion** in the floating dock to keep just a small animated orb, emblem, or character. Click it to expand the controls again. Choose your character in **Appearance → Companion style**. Your keyboard shortcut works while collapsed.

### Corrections that stick

In **Writing → Learn from my corrections**, enable local learning on Mac. Correct one spelling in the field where Say Less just pasted and keep that field focused for four seconds. The spelling pair appears in Writing. Choose **Keep now**, or repeat the same correction in another dictation to apply it automatically. You can remove learned rules anytime.

Learning is limited to supported text fields for 90 seconds after a paste. It stops when focus changes, skips password fields and auto-submit/reliable-paste modes, and stores only spelling pairs locally. It does not train a model or upload your edits.

Dictionary matching is now more conservative, so loosely similar names do not replace ordinary short words. Empty streaming results also retain the model for the fallback transcription attempt.
