### Record your screen

Say Less can now record your screen. One click on **Home → Record screen**, the new button on the floating dock, or **Record Screen** in the menu bar, and we capture your screen, your voice and your computer's sound into one video. Say Less itself stays out of the shot.

While it records you see a red dot and a timer. Click **Stop** and the video is saved to **Movies → Say Less**. Click **Show in Finder** to grab it.

Hands on the keyboard? Press your dictation shortcut and say **"Say less, start recording."** Say **"Say less, stop recording"** when you're done. Nothing gets typed. Turn the voice commands off in **Voice actions**.

The first time, macOS asks for Screen Recording permission. If you said no, we show you exactly where to turn it on. Screen recording needs macOS 15 or newer. Windows is coming soon.

### Avatars

Pick a character for your recording pill and dock. We added 16 painted avatars: six people, a tabby, a puppy, a fox, a frog, a bear, an owl, a robot, an alien and two silver chrome characters.

They still talk. The mouth opens with your voice and the eyes blink, just like before. Open **Appearance**, choose **Avatar** under the recording indicator, then pick one under **Pick a character**. Choose a **Ring color** to frame it in your own color.

The pictures come with the app, so they work offline. Want the old look? **Make your own** is still there with its styles, colors and hats.

### Set up your recording

Click **Setup** next to **Record screen** on Home, or the setup button on the floating dock, to choose what goes into the video:

- **What we record:** your whole screen (pick which one if you have more than one) or just one window.
- **Sound:** your microphone (and which one) and your computer's sound, each on or off.
- **Camera:** turn on **Show your face** and your camera appears in a small circle in the corner you pick, in the size you pick. It's in the video too. Drag the circle to move it while you record.
- **Quality:** 720p, 1080p or full size, at 30 or 60 frames a second. 1080p at 30 is the default and stays light on 8GB laptops.

Every change saves right away, so one click on **Record screen**, the dock button or **"Say less, start recording"** uses it. The first time you turn on the camera, macOS asks for Camera permission.
