### Readable dictation activity on Home

Home now labels your dictated words, daily streak, and estimated typing time saved. The note below the numbers explains the typing speed used for the estimate. These labels currently use English text in every interface language.

### Updates ready when you are

Say Less now downloads signed updates automatically. When a release is ready, choose **Restart to update** in the footer or update notice. You can keep working and restart later; finish any dictation or screen recording first. **Advanced → Automatic updates** turns checks and downloads off. Portable installs still offer a manual installer.
