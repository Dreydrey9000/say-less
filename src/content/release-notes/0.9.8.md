# Meet Say Less

Your local dictation app now has its own silver speech emblem. Look for **Say Less** in your Dock, menu bar, and app window.

Choose **Nemotron Streaming 3.5** in Models to try local streaming transcription. Grant Microphone and Accessibility access during setup, then set your shortcut in General.

Voice recognition runs on your computer. Keep optional cloud post-processing off when you want your transcript to stay local.
