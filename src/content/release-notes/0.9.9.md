# Write less. Say more.

**Voice snippets are here.** Open Writing in the sidebar, save a cue such as “my booking link,” and add the text it should insert. Try it with the built-in preview before using it in another app.

Snippets stay on your computer. When one matches, optional AI cleanup is skipped to preserve your saved text. Snippets support plain text and line breaks.

**Controls are easier to use.** Dropdowns support native keyboard navigation, switches have clear accessible names, primary actions use the silver finish, and settings rearrange at narrower widths. Open Details under a setting to read what it does.

Your personal dictionary and filler-word cleanup are also available in Writing.
