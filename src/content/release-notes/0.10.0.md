# Your voice. Your look.

![Say Less Appearance with color presets and floating dock control](/release-notes/appearance-0.10.0.png)

**Make it yours.** Open Appearance to choose Acid lime, Candy pink, Pool blue, Sunset, Chrome, or your own color. Show the floating dock to keep Record and Stop above your apps. Drag its grip to move it; the X hides it.

**Open apps with your voice.** In Voice actions, save an installed Mac app or a web link and give it a cue. Enable actions, then dictate “Say Less” followed by that exact cue. “Open now” tests the destination. Ordinary dictation and saved snippets never become executable commands.

**Bring your Wispr library.** Import from Wispr can preview your local dictionary and snippets, or read Wispr-format CSV/JSON files. Confirm the preview to add them; existing entries are preserved. History text is optional and appears in a separate archive. Audio, screenshots, account settings, shortcuts, and shared permissions are not migrated.

**Choose a writing style.** Writing now has local casing and punctuation rules, including per-app overrides. These are deterministic formatting rules, not AI tone rewriting. Snippets retain their saved formatting.
