### Studio hotkeys work from a closed app too

`/Applications/Say Less.app/Contents/MacOS/handy --open-studio titles` (or
`image`, `recordings`, `screens`, `library`) now opens the Studio on that page
whether Say Less is running or closed, and brings Say Less to the front. Use it
from Hammerspoon, Raycast or Shortcuts. The earlier `open -a "Say Less" --args`
form never worked for a running app, so use this one.
