### Move the small dock where you want it

The small floating dock has a **drag grip** at the lower left. Hold it to move the dock. Click the **X** at the upper left to hide it, or click the middle to open the full dock.

![The small Say Less dock with a drag grip at the lower left and a hide button at the upper left](/release-notes/0.14.7/small-dock.png "Drag the six dots to move the small dock, or click X to hide it.")

To show it again, open **Appearance → Floating dock** and turn on **Show floating dock**. Say Less now keeps the dock inside the screen when it changes size or your display setup changes.
