### Your Studio actions, one tap away

Your first four voice actions now show as buttons on the floating dock, on the
large dock and on the small dock when you hover it. Set one up in **Voice
actions** with the new **Create (in Say Less)** type, and saying "Say Less,
title ideas" (or make an image, my recordings, read my screens, library) opens
the Studio on that page.

The Studio's first tab is now **Images**, and it no longer shows a second logo
or status dots inside the window.

If you have the microphone allowed but not Accessibility, Say Less now opens
in browse mode instead of the full-screen setup, so you can still reach the
Studio. Typing into other apps stays off until Accessibility is on.
