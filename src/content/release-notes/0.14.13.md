### Your creative tools, in one app

Open **Studio** in the Say Less sidebar for **Create**, **Titles**, **Videos**,
**Screens**, and **Library**. Studio and its runtime now update with Say Less.

![Studio inside Say Less, with five creative tools](/release-notes/0.14.13/studio.png)

Add a **Create (in Say Less)** action in **Voice actions** and choose its Studio
page. Your spoken cue or dock button opens that page in Say Less. The large
dock and the small dock's hover rail show your first four enabled actions.

Open **Studio connections** to review your image and cloud tools. Generation,
vision and cloud storage still need their connected tools/accounts. Automatic
uploads start off until you enable them after setup.

Existing libraries are imported without removing originals. Add screenshots
directly or gather Desktop screenshots while keeping the originals. Restarting
for an update waits for active Studio jobs and uploads.
