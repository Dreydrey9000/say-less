### Voice visuals

Your voice now has more ways to show up. Open **Appearance → Voice visuals** and pick what moves in the recording overlay: the classic **Bars**, a smooth **Squiggle** line that grows as you talk, or your own **Avatar**.

Build the avatar right there: a stick figure, person, cat, or dog, your own colors, and a cap, beanie, crown, headphones, or sunglasses. Tap **Test the voice** to watch it talk. Its mouth follows how loud you are, not the exact words.

Want it in the floating dock too? Set **Companion style → Talking avatar**. Turn on **Pause animations** or your system's Reduce Motion and it holds still.

### Say less, stress less

Open **Insights** in the sidebar to see what you keep saying. Say Less finds your recurring problems ("mentioned 27 times"), your recurring ideas, and the one thing to **fix first**. Tap any topic to see what you said and when. A search box finds anything you've dictated.

It's built from your dictation history on this computer. Nothing is uploaded.

Optional, and off until you choose them:

- **Summarize with AI** writes a short digest using the AI provider you already set up in Post Process. It sends only topic names and a few short quotes.
- **Read digest aloud** uses your computer's built-in voices.
- **Export notes nightly** saves daily Markdown notes plus an Insights note that open in Obsidian. Say Less only writes its own files.
- **Use with Claude** shows a one-line setup so Claude can search your history through a local, read-only connection.

### Share it

Know someone who types too much? **About → Share Say Less** copies the free download link, saylessvoice.com.

### Hold Fn to talk

On a Mac you can now hold **Fn** (the Globe key) to dictate, just like Wispr Flow. Option+Space still works too. If the emoji picker pops up, open **System Settings > Keyboard** and set **Press Globe key to** to **Do Nothing**. Change it any time in **General > Shortcuts**.
