### Voice visuals

Your voice now has more ways to show up. Open **Appearance → Voice visuals** and pick what moves in the recording overlay: the classic **Bars**, a smooth **Squiggle** line that grows as you talk, or your own **Avatar**.

Build the avatar right there: a stick figure, person, cat, or dog, your own colors, and a cap, beanie, crown, headphones, or sunglasses. Tap **Test the voice** to watch it talk. Its mouth follows how loud you are, not the exact words.

Want it in the floating dock too? Set **Companion style → Talking avatar**. Turn on **Pause animations** or your system's Reduce Motion and it holds still.
