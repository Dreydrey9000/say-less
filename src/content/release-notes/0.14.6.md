### Record your screen

Needs macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer.

![The recording card on Home, with the Record screen and Setup buttons](/release-notes/0.14.1/record-card.png "This is on Home.")

1. Start: click **Record screen** on Home. Click Stop when you're done.
2. Add your face: click **Show my face** on Home.
3. Find your video: click **Open recordings folder**. It opens the Say Less folder in Movies.

The first time, macOS asks for Screen Recording permission. Turn it on in System Settings, then click **Reopen Say Less**.

### What's better in this update

<!-- mac -->

- New: press **Control + Option + R** from any app to start or stop a screen recording. Change it right on the recording card on Home.
- The orb on Home, the floating dock and your avatar now move with your voice while you talk, and while you narrate a screen recording. The wave line now stretches with every word instead of barely moving.
- The dock's buttons now say **Talk** and **Screen**, so you can tell them apart.
- If you said no to a permission, the button opens the right page in System Settings.
- Screen recordings save reliably, even long ones: Say Less waits for the file to finish writing before it opens it.
- Your first 3 voice actions are now buttons on the floating dock. Click one to run it without saying a word.
<!-- /mac -->
- The Bot avatar shows it's listening before you say a word: its eyes turn into little voice bars the moment you start. Its happy ^^ face at the end is easier to see.
- Switches are easier to read. On is filled with your color.
- **Check for updates** is now a real button, bottom right of the window. When a new version is ready it turns green and says **Update now**.
- The recording pop-up no longer shows a stuck "Cancel recording" label over your voice line.
<!-- mac -->
- On a Mac older than macOS 15, we show how to check your version and update it.
<!-- /mac -->

### Avatars

Pick one of 16 painted characters for the bubble that shows while you talk, and for the dock. Open Appearance, choose Avatar, then pick one under **Pick a character**.
