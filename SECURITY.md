# Security

Please report security problems privately through GitHub:
https://github.com/Dreydrey9000/say-less/security/advisories/new

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability. Include the app version, your OS, and steps to reproduce. We aim to reply within 7 days.

Say Less transcribes audio on your computer. Audio is never uploaded. The optional AI cleanup feature, off by default, sends transcript text to the provider you configure with your own key.
