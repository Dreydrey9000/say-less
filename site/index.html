How it works

Three steps. The first one is the whole trick.

Step 1

Hold your shortcut

Option Space Windows: Ctrl + Space

Click into any text box, anywhere on your computer, and hold the keys. Or tap once to keep it listening.

Step 2

Talk like normal

No robot voice. Say "um," change your mind, keep going.

Step 3

Let go

A second later, the words are typed out for you.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Is it actually free?

Yes. No trial, no credit card, no account. It runs on your computer, so there's no server bill to pass on to you.

Will it run on my laptop?

It is built for everyday laptops, including ones with 8GB of RAM. The recommended speech models are small (about 600 MB) and run on the processor you already have.

Does it need internet?

Only once, to download the speech model from the open-source Handy project's model server the first time you open it. After that it works fully offline.

Why does my Mac ask for permissions?

Two of them. Microphone, so it can hear you. Accessibility, so it can type into other apps for you. It can't do its job without both.

Mac or Windows?

Both: Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, plus Windows 10 and 11. The Mac button is for Apple Silicon. Intel Mac and Windows on ARM installers are on the releases page.

Can it clean up my writing with AI?

Optional. If you have your own AI key, you can turn on extra cleanup. That step sends the text (never the audio) to the AI provider you choose. It's off unless you turn it on.

Where did it come from?

Say Less is built on Handy, an open-source dictation project. We added our own features and design on top, and the code is open under the MIT license.
